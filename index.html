This yes or no wheel makes quick decisions for you when you are stuck, offering yes/no, yes/no/maybe, straight 50/50, or weighted modes. It is a lighthearted, no-install way to settle everyday dilemmas — what to eat, whether to go out, which show to watch, or which option to try — by letting a single spin give you an instant, random answer you can react to right away.

How the Yes or No Wheel Works

When you spin, the tool picks a random outcome using your browser's random number generator and lands the wheel on the matching segment. In balanced 50/50 mode, yes and no each have exactly half the chance. Add "maybe" and the three options split evenly, while weighted mode lets you tilt the odds toward one answer when you already lean a certain way.

Because each spin is independent, previous results never influence the next one — the wheel has no memory. That randomness is the point: it removes overthinking and analysis paralysis by committing you to an answer you can react to.

Example 1: Can't decide whether to order takeout tonight? A single spin in 50/50 mode gives a clean yes or no with equal odds.
Example 2: Leaning toward "yes" but want a nudge? Use weighted mode to give yes a 70% chance and let the spin confirm your instinct.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the yes or no wheel truly random?

Yes. It uses your browser's random number generator, so each spin is genuinely unpredictable and, in 50/50 mode, gives yes and no an equal chance.

What is the difference between the modes?

The 50/50 mode gives two equal options, the yes/no/maybe mode adds a third even option, and weighted mode lets you make one answer more likely than the other.

Should I trust the wheel for real decisions?

It is best for low-stakes choices. A useful trick is to notice how you feel about the result — relief or disappointment often reveals what you actually wanted.

Does a past result change the next spin?

No. Every spin is independent, so a string of "yes" answers does not make "no" more likely on the next spin.
